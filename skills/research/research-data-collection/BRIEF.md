# Research Data Collection

Collect evidence, not raw dumps. Prefer public pages and official APIs; check
scope, robots, and terms; keep crawls narrow and rate-limited. Never bypass
paywalls, logins, captchas, access controls, or collect sensitive personal data
without explicit lawful authorization.

Return a compact research pack with objective, cited sources/access time/method,
facts separated from inference, relevant claims/offers/pricing/proof/audience
language, confidence, limitations, and next actions. Mark contact channels as
validated, user-provided, inferred, inaccessible, or pending; never invent them.
