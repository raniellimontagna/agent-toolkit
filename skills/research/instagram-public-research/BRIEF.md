# Instagram Public Research

Research only. Use public pages allowed by the terms, authorized Graph API data,
or user-provided exports. Never post, message, engage, delete, read DMs, scrape
login-only data, bypass access controls, or collect sensitive personal data.

For each source record URL, access method, time, and authorization/public status.
Separate visible facts from inference, mark unavailable fields and why, and
summarize content/positioning, landing-page implications, citations, and limits
instead of dumping captions or comments.
